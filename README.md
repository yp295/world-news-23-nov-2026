An entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 23 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123): one newspaper page that sums up Monday, 23 November 2026.

## The image

`news.png` is a 1024×1024 newspaper front page with the masthead **Monday 23 November 2026**, 563,876 bytes. It carries **37 stories in total — 13 numbered main cards and 24 further items** in the "ALSO ON MONDAY 23 NOVEMBER" list.

Around the cards the page has the devices that make a day's news checkable at a glance:

- **THE DAY IN UTC** — a timeline of seven timed events, from the 00:08 Bitung quake to the night-time IRGC tanker claim.
- **THE DAY IN NUMBERS** — six big figures, led by Nvidia's +31% and the estimated 700,000 Russian soldiers killed.
- **Region bar** — every story counted by region: Europe 16, Americas 8, Middle East 4, East Asia 2, Africa 2, Global 2, Oceania 1, SE Asia 1, South Asia 1.
- **WHERE IT HAPPENED** — a world map with 13 numbered pins and location labels.
- **DATE CHECK** — the stories deliberately left out because they belong to another day (19–22 November, plus two 24 November items), so nothing from a neighbouring day can sneak in as a 23 November story.
- **Labels on every card** — CONFIRMED, OFFICIAL, REPORTED or CLAIM — with the source named under each card.

Every story on the page was first reported on 23 November 2026. Where a story is an update to something that happened earlier (Dymer, Cikande, Merkury), the card says UPDATE and states what is new on 23 November.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) does one thing: it copies `news.png` byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`, verifying the file's SHA-256 fingerprint before and after the copy. Run it with:

```
python3 main.py
```

Two runs produce identical bytes. The expected fingerprint is recorded in the program itself.
