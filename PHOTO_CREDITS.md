# Photo credits — news.png

## Please read this first

All thirteen picture slots in the newspaper image use real photographs,
but they are **representative file photos of the correct subject — not
photographs of the exact moment on 23 November 2026**. None of them is
verified as taken on that day. Most of the day's events have no
publishable "moment" photograph at all: a board vote, an unverified
military claim, a virtual congress and a leaked dataset do not produce
one. The photo for each card is described honestly below, so nobody is
misled about what it shows. The photographs belong to the outlets and
agencies named; using them in a bounty entry carries the copyright and
originality risk that a fully code-drawn version does not have.
